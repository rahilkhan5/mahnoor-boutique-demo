# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Astro (static pages, fast on cheap phones, good for Google), small client scripts for the cart and checkout, hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Same stack as the other demo sites.

## Users

- **Real audience: clothing boutiques, home-based designers and small fashion brands in Pakistan** whom the software house shows this demo to, usually on a phone over WhatsApp or Instagram. They judge whether their own collection would look this good online and whether customers could order without messaging them.
- **Story audience: women in Pakistan shopping for lawn, everyday kurtas and wedding-season formals**, mostly on a phone, often buying for themselves and for family, comparing colours and prices and asking "is this stitched or unstitched, and will it reach Multan by Eid?".

## Product Purpose

A demonstration online store for a fictional Lahore boutique, built by the software house to show what a store from them looks like and does: a collection people want to browse, clear product pages, a cart, and a checkout that offers the payment methods Pakistani customers actually use. Success: a boutique owner asks for one like it.

## Positioning

A boutique's own store instead of selling only through Instagram DMs: real prices on every piece, sizes and stitching options shown up front, cash on delivery, JazzCash, Easypaisa or card at checkout, and delivery times by city, so customers order at midnight without waiting for a reply.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from Instagram and Facebook posts, WhatsApp shares and Google. Pakistani women's wear: unstitched three-piece lawn suits (shirt, dupatta, trouser), ready-to-wear kurtas, formals for mehndi and barat, seasonal drops (summer lawn, Eid, winter khaddar), prices in PKR, delivery across Pakistan by courier, cash on delivery as the default trust signal.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Everything is a demo: the boutique, products, prices, stock and reviews are made up and labelled as a demo where a visitor could mistake them for real.
- Product photos come from free stock libraries (Unsplash licence) and are credited.
- The cart works in the browser and stays on the device. Checkout is simulated: no payment is taken, no card details are sent or stored, and the JazzCash, Easypaisa and card steps say plainly that they are demos. Cash on delivery places a fake order with a fake order number.
- No real phone or WhatsApp numbers. Example numbers read 03XX XXXXXXX.
- English copy, with Urdu where it helps (fabric names, a few labels).

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real boutique, products, reviews or photos. Photos are free stock images; the collection is written for the demo.

## Product Principles

1. The clothes first: big, honest photos and the price right there.
2. No guessing: stitched or unstitched, sizes, fabric, what is in the box and delivery time are on the product page, not in DMs.
3. Pay the Pakistani way: cash on delivery, JazzCash, Easypaisa and card, all clear and equal.
4. Honest demo: nobody should think they bought something or paid.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable type on a phone, strong contrast on prices and buttons, real alt text describing each outfit, colour names written out (never colour alone), works on slow connections.
